# Review the Assistant's edits in the report before you accept them

The Assistant's suggestions now wait for you. Press Review to see exactly which words change in the report, pick the version you like, and accept it, with Undo always close by.

### New
- Each suggestion covers one Line. A change to two Lines comes as two cards, and you review and accept each one on its own, or accept them together with Accept all.
- For a trim or a rewording, the Assistant can offer up to three options. Option 1 is the one it recommends.
- Press Review and the report marks only the words that change: old words struck in red, new words in green. Turn Show changes off to read the report as it would read once accepted.
- When a request could mean different things, the Assistant asks you a short question with a few answers to pick from, right where you type.
- A Line over its CRA limit shows how far over it is. Click it, then "Ask the Assistant to shorten it".
- A new conversation offers to shorten any Line that is over its limit.

### Improved
- After you accept, the card turns into a short line, and an Undo bar sits above the message box. Open any accepted card to undo it later.
- When something can't be reviewed, accepted or undone, the chat says why in plain words, with a way to Version history when you need it.
- The Assistant's header shows the conversation's name, with buttons for your chat history and a new conversation.
