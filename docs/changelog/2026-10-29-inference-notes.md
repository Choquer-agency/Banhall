# Drafts mark what the AI inferred, so you know which sentences to check

When a draft says something the sources do not state, it now tells you so, right after the sentence, instead of writing it as fact.

### New
- A sentence the AI inferred from the sources, rather than found in them, is followed by a note saying what it rests on, shown as a teal highlight in the report. Check the sentence, then delete the note to keep it (one Backspace at the note removes it), or remove the sentence.
- Where the sources give nothing to go on, the draft leaves a gap for the missing fact instead of making something up.
- Before you export, the export window lists every gap and inference still in the report, each with a Show button that takes you to it.

### Improved
- Inference notes never count toward a Line's words or form lines, and the Word file highlights any you have not checked yet, next to the gaps.
- The Assistant keeps inference notes when it edits a passage, and can add them when it writes something the sources do not state.
