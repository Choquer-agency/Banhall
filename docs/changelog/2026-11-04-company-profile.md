# A full profile for every company: summary, files, people and activity

Each company page now opens on an overview with a short summary of what the company does and all its projects in one table, with tabs for its files, its contacts, what changed, and its details.

### New
- A company page has tabs: Overview, Files, People, Activity and Details. The page remembers the tab you were on, so a link opens it again.
- Switch to another company from the company's name at the top of the page. Type to find one.
- The top of the page shows the company's website, industry, when it became a client, and the people at Banhall working on its projects.
- A short company summary is written by AI from the company's website after it is read. Managers and Admins can edit it, and their words are never replaced. The card says whether the AI wrote it or who last edited it.
- Overview lists the company's projects in one table, 10 at a time, with a search, filters for stage, fiscal year and owner, and a sort. Click a row to open the project.
- People lists the company's own contacts, with their role, email and phone, and its main contact. Consultants, Managers and Admins can add, edit and remove them.
- Activity shows what changed on the company and its projects, day by day: files added or removed, projects started or moved to a new stage, and drafts started. Filter it by person or by kind of change.
- Details lists the company's name, industry, usual year end and website, who added it, when it last changed, and the names merged into it. Managers and Admins can read the website again from here.

### Improved
- Files is easier to work with: preview a file before you open it, set its category and fiscal year, select several files to download at once, and drop files anywhere on the page to add them. Managers and Admins can replace a file with a newer version.
