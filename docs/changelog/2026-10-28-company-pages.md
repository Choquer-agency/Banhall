# Every company gets its own page, and you can edit a company's details

Companies in the left rail now opens a list of every company, and each company has a page with its details, its projects by fiscal year and its files.

### New
- Click Companies in the rail to see every company with its number of projects, its fiscal year end and when it last changed. Search the list by name, and click a company to open its page.
- A company's page shows its fiscal year end, industry and number of projects at the top, then its projects by fiscal year and its files. New project there starts a project for that company.
- Managers and Admins can edit a company's name, fiscal year end and industry with Edit company. A new name moves every project of the company to it. If another company already has that name, you are asked to merge the two instead.
- An old link to a company that was merged opens the company it was merged into, with a short note saying what happened.

### Improved
- On the Projects page, the arrow beside a company's name opens its company page. A company's files now live on its company page only.
- A company with no projects yet says so plainly and offers a New project button for that company.
