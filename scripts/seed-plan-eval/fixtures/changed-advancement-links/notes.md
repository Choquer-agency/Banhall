# Cold-Water Biofilter Control: Trial Summary, Fiscal 2026

Marrowgate Aquatics Inc., Kelvale, Nova Scotia. Prepared by Tomas Reinholt (Biological Systems) and Farah Okonkwo-Lind (Controls). Period: July 1 2025 to June 30 2026. Issued June 30 2026.

## Pilot setup

Four independent pilot loops (A to D). Each loop has a 6 m3 fish tank and a 1.2 m3 moving bed biofilter with K-style plastic carriers (specific surface about 800 m2/m3). Stock: rainbow trout, 180 to 420 g average weight during the year, stocking density 35 to 45 kg/m3. Intake water ranged from 5.8 C in February to 11.4 C in October. Target water quality: TAN under 1.0 mg/L, nitrite under 0.3 mg/L, alkalinity over 120 mg/L as CaCO3.

The project covers three separate technological uncertainties:

- U1: how to shorten biofilter nitrification start-up at water temperatures below 10 C.
- U2: how to keep total ammonia nitrogen from spiking after feeding surges.
- U3: whether dissolved-oxygen and ammonia sensors can stay accurate in biofilm-heavy water.

## Trial summary

Trial 1 (U1), September 8 to November 13 2025, loops A, B and C at 8 C. Loop A was unseeded with ammonium chloride dosing, loop B had 10 percent unacclimated seed from a 16 C system, loop C had 10 percent seed acclimated from 14 C to 8 C at 1 C every 4 days. Days to full nitrification were 66 for A, 47 for B and 31 for C. The nitrite stall lasted 19 days in B and 6 days in C.

Trial 2 (U1), January 5 to February 18 2026, loops C and D at 6 C. Acclimated seed at 5 percent in one loop and 15 percent in the other. Days to full nitrification were 44 at 5 percent and 29 at 15 percent.

Trial 3 (U2), November 24 2025 to January 30 2026, loops A and B at 9 C. Feeding at 1.5 percent body weight per day in two meals (08:00 and 15:00). Under pH-setpoint dosing at pH 7.2, peak TAN reached 2.3 mg/L about 3 hours after the morning feed, alkalinity fell from 150 to 95 mg/L and pH dipped to 6.9. Under feed-forward dosing at 0.25 kg NaHCO3 per kg feed, 45 minutes ahead, peak TAN fell to 1.2 mg/L and alkalinity stayed above 130 mg/L. Bicarbonate use rose by about 6 percent over the pH-setpoint baseline.

Trial 4 (U2), March 2 to April 13 2026, loops A, B and D at 9 to 10 C, with feed-forward dosing on all three loops. Media fill ratios of 40, 55 and 65 percent gave peak TAN of 1.2, 0.8 and 1.1 mg/L. At 65 percent, carriers clumped in the corners and DO fell to 5.1 mg/L in dead zones, against 8.5 mg/L in the bulk.

Trial 5 (U3), December 1 2025 to May 29 2026, sensors on loops A and B. Direct in-tank sensors cleaned weekly were compared with a bypass loop with a 50 micron screen and an air blast every 6 hours. Lab reference samples were taken 3 times per week, 74 samples in total. Direct DO read 8 percent low by day 10 and direct ammonium drift was 0.4 mg/L per week. In the bypass loop, DO stayed within 3 percent for 28 days and ammonium drift was about 0.1 mg/L per week. With weekly automatic two-point checks added, ammonium error was within 0.12 mg/L over 4 weeks.

## Results by uncertainty

### U1: cold-water start-up

Stepwise acclimation of seed media roughly halves start-up time compared with unacclimated seed at 8 C (31 against 47 days). Nitrite oxidizers are the main bottleneck, shown by the long nitrite stall in the unacclimated loop. At 6 C, about 15 percent acclimated seed is needed to reach full nitrification in under 5 weeks. Gains flatten above 10 to 15 percent. Client farm application in March 2026 reached full nitrification in 34 days at 7 to 8 C.

### U2: feeding surge spikes

Two separate advancements came from this work.

1. Feed-forward alkalinity dosing tied to feeding events (0.25 kg NaHCO3 per kg feed, 45 minutes before each meal, pH loop as trim only) keeps alkalinity and pH up through the ammonia pulse and cuts peak TAN from 2.3 to 1.2 mg/L.
2. A 55 percent media fill ratio buffers the spike, lowering peak TAN to 0.8 mg/L and flattening the curve. A 65 percent fill makes it worse through clumping and low-oxygen dead zones. This is a separate mechanism from the dosing rule.

Combined, the pilot held TAN under 1.0 mg/L at 1.5 percent body weight feeding without enlarging the biofilter.

### U3: sensor accuracy in biofilm-heavy water

A screened bypass loop with 6-hourly air blast and weekly automatic two-point checks keeps DO within 3 percent and ammonium within 0.12 mg/L for 4 weeks. Direct in-tank sensors did not last 2 weeks. Ammonium membranes still need replacement about every 3 months.

## Open items

- Adjust the feed-forward rule for fish size (fish under 150 g).
- Extend ammonium membrane life past 3 months.
- Add TAN feedback trim to the dosing loop once ammonium readings are trusted.
