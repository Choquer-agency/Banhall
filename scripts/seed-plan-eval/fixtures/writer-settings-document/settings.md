# PD Writing Customized Settings

Velloway Panel Finishing Ltd., low-temperature powder coating of MDF panels, fiscal 2026 (July 1 2025 to June 30 2026). Prepared by Priya Halvorsen, SR&ED writer, after the interview. These settings apply to every Line of this project description. Where they differ from the house defaults, these settings win.

## 1. Core variable glossary

Use these exact terms for the project's named variables. Never use the alternatives listed after each one, even where the client uses them.

- film build: the cured coating thickness, in microns. Never write DFT, dry film thickness, film thickness or coating thickness.
- panel surface temperature: the measured temperature at the MDF surface, in degrees C. Never write substrate temperature, substrate temp, board temperature or part temperature.
- cure window: the range of panel surface temperature that gives full cure with no outgassing defects. Never write bake window or oven window.
- edge coverage: the film build on the routed edges. Never write edge wrap or edge build.
- outgassing defects: the pinholes and blisters left when gas leaves the MDF during cure. Never write pinholes, pinholing, blisters or blistering on their own. Call them outgassing defects.

## 2. Banned words and phrases

Never use these words or phrases, in any form:

- successfully
- in order to
- optimize (also optimise, optimized, optimization and the like)
- proprietary
- trial and error
- breakthrough
- industry-leading

## 3. Required openings

- Line 242, objective statement: open it with these exact words: "The aim of this work was to". This replaces the house opener for the objective.
- Line 242, uncertainty statement: open the statement of the uncertainties with these exact words: "It was not known at the outset whether".

## 4. Word caps

- Line 242: no more than 260 words.

- Line 246: no more than 260 words.

## 5. Claim exclusion list

Do not claim these as SR&ED. Do not describe them anywhere in the project description, not even to say they are excluded:

1. The powder booth extraction upgrade (new extraction fans and cartridge filters, spring 2026).
2. Colour matching of the customer's catalogue shades.

## 6. Style

Write in the third person throughout. Never use we, our, ours, us or ourselves. Name the company or say the team.

## 7. Findings confidence

- High: outgassing defects start at a panel surface temperature of about 118 to 122 C on this board, whatever the dwell time.
- High: the fast-catalysed powder reached full cure at a panel surface temperature of 120 C.
- Medium: edge coverage on deep cove profiles. The pilot met 60 microns on most panels, not all of them.

## 8. Most defensible storyline

Lead with the conflict. Making MDF conductive and curing the powder both need heat, and heat drives gas out of the board. The team separated conductivity from heat with an edge sealer, then brought full cure below the outgassing onset with a faster powder. Keep the deep cove profile open as the remaining uncertainty.
