# Measurement protocol — Paired Comparison and success metrics

Realizes CAP-16; defines the Success signal.

## Projects
At least five: 25001 (development project, Reference PD exists, reported separately and excluded from SM-1/SM-2); at least two projects untouched during Brief and Self-check development; at least one 100–200-hour project; Reference PDs from Larry or Tracy.

## Inputs
Identical files and settings text into ChatGPT (Sol, a fresh ChatGPT project with the same files) and into Banhall (Sol). Record product and model versions and the model-equivalence caveat on every record; a Sonnet/Opus arm is optional.

## Blinding
A third person, not the judge, strips both drafts to identically formatted plain text (no Compliance Notes, Brief, glossary callouts or tool-specific headings); drafts labelled A/B, order randomised per project; judge is the writer who owns the project; Michael as second judge where available.

## Counts
- Deviations: counted by the writer, same manual method for both drafts, against their settings; the Deviation Inventory (CAP-12) is not used for either draft.
- Corrections-to-acceptable: revision rounds until the writer says "acceptable".
- LLM self-scores are never an outcome.

## Metrics
- **SM-1** Paired Comparison win: on ≥ 4 non-development projects (≥ 1 small), Banhall preferred in ≥ 3, with ≤ half the Deviations of the ChatGPT draft. Validates CAP-1–10, CAP-16.
- **SM-2** Corrections-to-acceptable ≤ 1 on ≥ 3 of 4 non-development projects; the 16-item harness fixture reports 16/16 every run. Validates CAP-12–14.
- **SM-3** Zero writer-authored artifacts offline after launch (weekly self-report, Larry and Tracy, through September). Validates CAP-1–3, CAP-14.
- **SM-4** Zero silent overrides: every unapplied instruction appears in a Compliance Note. Validates CAP-6–8.
- **SM-5** ChatGPT drafting drops week over week; "comparing to my own reference" counts as drafting. Validates CAP-15 and the Why.

## Counter-metrics (never optimise)
- **SM-C1** Draft length must not rise toward the caps; numeric self-score is never a target.
- **SM-C2** Time and cost within 2x today's single-mode run; repair-budget overruns recorded in the scorecard.
- **SM-C3** QA gate failures and fabrication flags must not increase; the Brief must not introduce facts.
- **SM-C4** Time-to-first-draft with no Brief interaction must not exceed SM-C2.
- **SM-C5** Brief-edit burden (share of generations with a substantially rewritten derived entry: Storyline edit distance above threshold, or more than a third of entries changed) must fall over September.
