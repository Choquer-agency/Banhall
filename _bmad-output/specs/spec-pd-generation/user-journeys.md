# User journeys

Load-bearing for story breakdown and acceptance. UJ-1 and UJ-2 are captured from Larry's account on the 2026-09-09 call; UJ-3 to UJ-5 are authored scenarios derived from it.

- **UJ-1 — Larry, first draft for a 1300-hour project (CAP-1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11).** One long Transcript for 25001, his Writer Profile, his own Storyline. He drops the Transcript, title, context, two spreadsheets; pastes his Storyline into the Brief; picks Sol; generates. The Brief fills in around his Storyline (Claim Exclusions, Confidence Map, Glossary Terms, each with its source passage); he changes one term. Sections arrive in his Build Order with Compliance Notes showing what was checked and repaired. Climax: fewer Deviations than his ChatGPT draft; one vocabulary; the 246 note reads "applied up to the CRA limit of 50 lines; the limit is locked." Edge: an over-budget Transcript is condensed and the Brief and Confidence Map say so.
- **UJ-2 — Larry, sixteen Deviations in one pass (CAP-12, 13, 14, 15).** He asks for the Deviation Inventory (14 rule Deviations), adds two content corrections, says "bring all sixteen into alignment." One Coordinated Revision: 14 resolved, 1 blocked (date absent from the Transcript), 1 conflicting (would exceed the locked 244 word cap; alternative offered). He applies. Edge: "how can I help you converge?" returns the two interview facts it lacks, never a request for a document.
- **UJ-3 — Tracy, three Transcripts, no Writer Profile (CAP-1, 6, 8, 11).** One Storyline reconciled across three interviews; disagreements visible in the Confidence Map; House Rules apply in full and the Brief says no Writer Profile is applied.
- **UJ-4 — Michael runs the Paired Comparison (CAP-16).** Identical inputs into ChatGPT (Sol) and Banhall; a third person strips both to plain text; Larry rates blind and counts Deviations and Corrections-to-acceptable the same way for both; results stored.
- **UJ-5 — Larry, a 150-hour project in an afternoon (CAP-1, 5, 13).** One short Transcript, no Reference PD; the Brief derives the Storyline; one Coordinated Revision later the PD is acceptable without ChatGPT. Small projects are the tool's default home and the comparison set includes one.
