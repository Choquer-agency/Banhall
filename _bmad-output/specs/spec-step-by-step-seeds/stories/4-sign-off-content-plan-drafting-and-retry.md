## Intent

**Problem:** Ready seed generations cannot freeze approved decisions, enter prose drafting, or retry a failed draft from the same plan.

**Approach:** Add CAP-12/CAP-13 through `generations.ts` and extend the existing ordered chain to consume one frozen Summary and emit AD-37 compliance evidence.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:** One mutation requires `report.editProse`, seeds workflow, active `awaiting_input`, expected version and the query's transaction-local `readSeedReadiness`. Freeze selections, reference closure, Skips, Brief and settings in `orderShownSet` order; terminalize pending attempts; then link one non-ghost candidate to the Build Order chain. Late callbacks never become current; after sign-off seed mutations/dispatch refuse as closed. Content precedence is Locked Rules > signed-off selections > Brief; `writer_asserted` is Writer's Notes, Glossary Terms change wording only, and style precedence is unchanged. Keep one Self-check call per Section, the existing report writer and post-QA.

**Ask First:** Changes to plan meaning, precedence, conflict handling, lineage, lifecycle, permissions, call count, or report writer need an approved domain amendment.

**Never:** Mutate signed-off plans; put Seeds/Summary in prose, Brief, or Proposal; read live inputs on retry; fork drafting or Self-check; repair confirmed exclusion conflicts; change legacy, single, or compare. Story 7 owns the six-fixture evaluator.
