Line 242 — Scientific/Technological Uncertainty

Client Co's order-routing platform could not guarantee that two concurrent price updates would resolve to the same final value once the caching layer sat in front of the primary database. The team did not know, at the outset, which of three consistency models — last-write-wins, vector-clock reconciliation, or a central lock — would hold under production load without reintroducing the latency the caching layer was meant to remove.

Existing literature on cache coherence assumes a single writer or a tolerance for stale reads; neither held for Client Co's pricing feed, where a stale read could route an order at the wrong price. The uncertainty was whether a consistency model existed that satisfied both constraints at once, and the team could not resolve this analytically — it required building and load-testing each candidate against replayed production traffic.
